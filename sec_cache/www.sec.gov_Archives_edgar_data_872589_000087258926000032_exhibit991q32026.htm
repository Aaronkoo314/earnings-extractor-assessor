Regeneron Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results
•Third quarter 2026 revenues increased 36% to $5.1 billion versus third quarter 2025
•Dupixent® global net sales (recorded by Sanofi) increased 35% to $6.5 billion
•EYLEA HD® U.S. net sales increased 62% to $699 million
•Libtayo® global net sales increased 51% to $550 million
•GAAP EPS increased 35% to $18.45; non-GAAP EPS(a) increased 70% to $20.07
•Expanded collaboration with Sanofi, adding multiple next-generation, long-acting immunology antibodies; $1.0 billion up-front to be recognized as collaboration revenue in fourth quarter 2026 with potential to receive up to an additional $7.0 billion in future milestones
•Pasatru™ (garetosmab) approved by FDA for fibrodysplasia ossificans progressiva (FOP)
Tarrytown, New York (October 30, 2026) -- Regeneron Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: REGN) today announced financial results for the third quarter of 2026 and provided a business update.
"This was an outstanding quarter for Regeneron, with Dupixent, EYLEA HD, and Libtayo each achieving record net sales," said Leonard S. Schleifer, M.D., Ph.D., Board co-Chair, President and Chief Executive Officer of Regeneron. "Our pipeline of about 50 product candidates is rapidly advancing. We expect an FDA decision for cemdisiran in generalized myasthenia gravis next month; our Phase 3 programs in multiple myeloma, lymphoma, anticoagulation, and obesity continue to advance; and we have generated encouraging early data in MASH, POTS, and ovarian cancer. Finally, we expanded our alliance with Sanofi to include four Regeneron-invented, next-generation, long-acting antibodies designed to build on the success of Dupixent."

Business Highlights
Key Pipeline Progress
Regeneron has approximately 50 product candidates in clinical development, including a number of marketed products for which it is investigating additional indications. Updates from the clinical pipeline include:
Libtayo (cemiplimab)
•In August 2026, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) in Japan approved Libtayo as an adjuvant treatment for cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (CSCC) and for the treatment of metastatic or locally advanced CSCC. These indications are already approved in the United States and European Union (EU).
•In October 2026, the European Medicines Agency’s (EMA) Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) adopted a positive opinion recommending the approval of an alternative dosing schedule for Libtayo across all approved indications. The positive opinion supports reducing Libtayo dosing frequency from every three weeks to every six weeks after the first 12 weeks of treatment. The European Commission (EC) is expected to make a final decision in the coming months. There is also a supplemental Biologics License Application (sBLA) under review by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), which has a target action date in March 2027.
•In October 2026, the Company announced longer-term data which showed increasing and clinically meaningful overall survival benefit for Libtayo as an adjuvant treatment for patients with CSCC. The results were presented at the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO) 2026 Meeting.
Other Oncology Programs
•The FDA accepted for review the resubmission of the BLA for Ordspono™ (odronextamab) in relapsed or refractory diffuse large B-cell lymphoma (DLBCL), which has a target action date in February 2027.
•The Company announced that ubamatamab (MUC16 and CD3 bispecific antibody) showed a high rate of durable responses in an initial study in patients with advanced low-grade serous ovarian cancer (LGSOC). The data are from an ongoing Phase 1/2 trial and were presented at the International Gynecologic Cancer Society (IGCS) 2026 Annual Global Meeting. Based on these preliminary trial results, the Company has been enrolling a single-arm, potentially registrational LGSOC trial and is planning to initiate a confirmatory Phase 3 trial in this setting.
Pasatru (garetosmab)
•In August 2026, the FDA approved Pasatru to reduce formation of new heterotopic ossification lesions and clinician-assessed flare-ups in adults with FOP. A regulatory application is also under review in the EU.
•The Company announced positive 84-week results from the Phase 3 trial for Pasatru in adults with FOP. These longer-term findings demonstrated sustained efficacy in halting new, rogue bone growth and in significantly reducing disease flare-ups, and were presented at the American Society for Bone and Mineral Research (ASBMR) Annual Meeting.
Other Programs
•A three-arm, placebo-controlled Phase 3 study was initiated to evaluate cenvacibart (an antibody to Factor XI (catalytic domain)), and amrecibart (an antibody to Factor XI (A2 domain)), individually, for the prevention of venous thromboembolism in patients with a peripherally inserted central catheter (PICC).
2

•The Company has an ongoing Phase 1/2 study for its RNAi therapy targeting CIDEB. Interim results from this randomized, placebo-controlled, first-in-human trial in adults with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) showed that a single dose was generally well tolerated and reduced liver fat by approximately 70% on average from baseline at 12 weeks, with effects sustained through 24 weeks of follow-up. The Company plans to present interim results at the American Association for the Study of Liver Diseases (AASLD) Meeting in November 2026 and to initiate a Phase 3 study in metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH)/MASLD in the fourth quarter of 2026.
•The Company plans to report results from the Phase 2 study for baloncibart (NPR1 antagonist antibody) in postural orthostatic tachycardia syndrome (POTS) at the American Autonomic Society (AAS) Meeting in November 2026. Additionally, the Company plans to initiate a Phase 3 study in POTS in the first half of 2027, pending regulatory feedback.
•The Company announced new results from the Phase 2 COURAGE trial evaluating trevogrumab (myostatin antibody) for its potential to prevent lean mass and muscle loss during GLP-1 receptor agonist-induced weight loss in people living with obesity. The results of the trial were presented at the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD) Annual Meeting.
•A second Phase 3 study was initiated for REGN1908-1909, an investigational treatment for cat allergy.
Corporate Updates
•In October 2026, the Company and Sanofi amended their antibody collaboration, adding multiple next-generation, long-acting Regeneron-invented antibodies to the alliance. Under the terms of the agreement, the Company received an up-front payment of $1.0 billion, and is entitled to receive up to an additional $7.0 billion in the aggregate upon the achievement of certain development, regulatory, and sales-based milestones. The companies will equally share development and commercialization costs for the new programs, and, if commercialized, share any future profits equally on a global basis.
•In October 2026, the Company announced an agreement with the Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority (BARDA) to provide additional supply of Inmazeb® for U.S. preparedness against Ebola disease. Under the terms of the agreement, the Company expects to deliver a pre-specified number of treatment doses of Inmazeb each year to the U.S. government beginning in 2027 through 2031.

About Regeneron
Regeneron is a leading biotechnology company that invents, develops, and commercializes life-transforming medicines for people with serious diseases. Founded and led by physician-scientists, Regeneron's unique ability to repeatedly and consistently translate science into medicine has led to numerous approved treatments and product candidates in development, most of which were homegrown in Regeneron's laboratories. Regeneron's medicines and pipeline are designed to help patients with eye diseases, allergic and inflammatory diseases, cancer, cardiovascular and metabolic diseases, neurological diseases, hematologic conditions, infectious diseases, and rare diseases.
Regeneron pushes the boundaries of scientific discovery and accelerates drug development using its proprietary technologies, such as VelociSuite®, which produces optimized fully human antibodies and new classes of bispecific antibodies. Regeneron is shaping the next frontier of medicine with data-powered insights from the Regeneron Genetics Center® and pioneering genetic medicine platforms, enabling Regeneron to identify innovative targets and complementary approaches to potentially treat or cure diseases.
For more information, please visit www.regeneron.com or follow Regeneron on LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, or X.
