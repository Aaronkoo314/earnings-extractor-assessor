Xperi Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Accelerating Growth in Advertising and Related Revenue Drove Media Platform Revenue

to Double Year-Over-Year

 

TiVo One Monthly Active Users Grew to 6.8 Million – a Year-Over-Year Increase of 40%

 

MediaMarktSaturn, Europe’s Largest Consumer Electronics Retailer, Selected TiVo OS as the Operating System Partner for its Private-Label TV Brand PEAQ

 

Company Narrows Annual Revenue Outlook and Raises Adjusted EBITDA Margin and Operating Cash Flow Outlook

 

Robert Andersen, CFO, Will Retire from Xperi in 2027 as Part of a Planned Transition

 

San Jose, Calif. (November 4, 2026) – Xperi Inc. (NYSE: XPER) (the “Company” or “Xperi”), a media and entertainment technology company that invents, develops, and delivers technologies that enable extraordinary experiences, today announced third quarter 2026 financial results for the period ended September 30, 2026.

“As expected, our success in building our TiVo One footprint has begun to drive substantial growth in advertising and related revenue. The selection of TiVo’s Operating System by MediaMarktSaturn, the largest electronics retailer across Europe with more than 1,000 stores, demonstrates the benefit of our independent media platform solution, the strength of our retail media product offerings, and our unique competitive position in the market. Given the strong strategic execution year-to-date, we are pleased to raise our full-year outlook range for adjusted EBITDA margin and operating cash flow,” said Jon Kirchner, chief executive officer of Xperi. “Additionally, today we are announcing that our CFO, Robert Andersen, will retire from Xperi as part of a planned transition. Robert will continue in his current role until his successor is on board. On behalf of the Board of Directors and all team members at Xperi, I would like to thank Robert for his many years of dedicated leadership at the Company.”

Recent Key Operating Achievements

Media Platform

Expanded footprint, product enhancements, and growing advertising partnerships continue to accelerate advertising and related revenue

 

1

 

•
Advertising and Related revenue growth accelerated during the quarter, achieving approximately 280% growth in the quarter as the primary driver of Media Platform revenue doubling year-over-year. On a year-to-date basis, Advertising and Related revenue grew 90% versus the prior year.
•
TiVo was selected by MediaMarktSaturn, Europe’s largest consumer electronics retailer, as its Smart TV operating system partner, including for its private-label TV brand, which is expected to create a new retail media and commerce opportunity across the TV experience. This strategic win expands TiVo OS to 11 OEM design partnerships and is expected to strengthen TiVo’s position as the leading independent Smart TV OS in the European market.
•
U.S. distribution for TiVo OS expanded with Element Smart TVs becoming available across major U.S. online retailers.
•
TiVo One ARPU of $7.65 increased sequentially from last quarter by 14% and TiVo One Monthly Active Users finished the quarter at 6.8 million.

 

Connected Car

Continued growth in AutoStage platform footprint and new automotive OEM programs are contributing to advertising and related revenue

 

•
DTS AutoStage footprint momentum continued with more than 18 million cars shipped to date across 13 automotive brands, increasing platform coverage to more than 150 countries.
•
Expanded the DTS AutoStage Broadcaster Portal from 300 to 550 U.S. metro areas and entered into data monetization agreements with eight additional broadcasters. Revenue from the portal is advertising-related and recognized within Media Platform.
•
Signed a multi-year HD Radio program with a large Asian Tier 1 supplier that supports multiple automotive OEMs in Japan and North America, illustrating the continued importance of HD Radio in enabling additional shipment growth.
•
Expanded HD Radio vehicle availability with new model launches across Hyundai, Kia, Volkswagen, Toyota, Honda, and Subaru.

 

Pay TV

Continued revenue growth in video-over-broadband, along with key design wins, demonstrates partner commitment to the TiVo platform

•
Extended multi-year video-over-broadband agreements with Hotwire and Armstrong, including expanded commercial commitments.
•
Expanded TiVo video-over-broadband momentum in Canada with four new internet service provider (ISP) customer wins at FlexNetworks, IGS Hawkesbury, Uptern Communications, and Xplore.
•
The third quarter ended with 3.42 million global IPTV subscriber households.

 

Consumer Electronics

Continued to secure long-term renewal commitments to our technologies

•
Renewed a multi-year IMAX® Enhanced agreement with TCL, a top-three global Smart TV manufacturer, reinforcing TCL’s long-term commitment to the premium entertainment format and expanding DTS:X’s reach across a leading Smart TV OEM.
•
Executed DTS:X agreements with Holatek for projectors, Trinnov for AV preamplifiers and processors, and Evolution Digital for streaming devices, demonstrating the technology’s versatility and expanded presence across the home entertainment ecosystem.

Definition for TiVo One Monthly Active User

Xperi defines a “TiVo One Monthly Active User” as a unique device that has connected to the TiVo video service, which includes the TiVo One advertising platform, at least once within the last 30 days. The TiVo One advertising platform integrates with the device’s operating system on certain “Powered by TiVo” devices, including smart TVs and video-over-broadband products.

Calculation of Average Revenue Per User for TiVo One

Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) for TiVo One is calculated by dividing advertising and related revenue (excluding automotive-related revenue) for the trailing four quarters by the average number of TiVo One Monthly Active Users during that same period. This metric helps investors and management measure how effectively the Company monetizes its media platform through advertising and data.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

In addition to disclosing financial results calculated in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (“GAAP”), the Company’s press release contains non-GAAP financial measures, including Non-GAAP Operating Income/(Loss), Non-GAAP Net Income/(Loss), Non-GAAP Net Income/(Loss) Per Share, Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA, Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Free Cash Flow, and Non-GAAP Tax Expense.

Non-GAAP Operating Income/(Loss) is defined as GAAP Operating Income/(Loss), less the impact of stock-based compensation; amortization of intangible assets; transaction, integration and restructuring costs; severance and retention costs; and other items not indicative of our ongoing operating performance.

 

Non-GAAP Net Income/(Loss) is defined as GAAP Net Income/(Loss) excluding the impact of stock-based compensation; amortization of intangible assets; transaction, integration and restructuring costs; severance and retention costs; and other items not indicative of our ongoing operating performance; and related tax effects for each adjustment.

 

Non-GAAP Net Income/(Loss) Per Share is defined as Non-GAAP Net Income/(Loss) divided by Non-GAAP weighted average shares outstanding - diluted.

 

Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA is defined as GAAP Net Income/(Loss), less the impact of interest expense; provision for income taxes; stock-based compensation; depreciation expense; amortization of intangible assets; amortization of capitalized cloud computing costs; transaction, integration and restructuring costs; severance and retention costs; and other items not indicative of our ongoing operating performance.

 

Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA Margin is defined as Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA divided by total revenue.

 

Free Cash Flow is defined as net cash from operating activities, less cash investments for capitalized internal-use software and purchases of property and equipment.

 

Non-GAAP Tax Expense is defined as the GAAP provision for income taxes, adjusted to reflect the net direct and indirect income tax effects of the various non-GAAP pretax adjustments.
