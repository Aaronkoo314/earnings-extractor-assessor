Williams-Sonoma, Inc. announces strong third quarter 2026 results
Q3 comparable brand revenue +5.1%; net revenue growth of +5.8%
GAAP operating margin of 18.9%; non-GAAP operating margin of 17.4%
GAAP diluted EPS of $2.38, growth of +21.4%; non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.19, growth of +11.7%
Reiterates full-year 2026 outlook
San Francisco, CA, November 19, 2026 – Williams-Sonoma, Inc. (NYSE: WSM) today announced operating results for the third quarter ended November 1, 2026 versus the third quarter ended November 2, 2025.
“We delivered another strong quarter in Q3, with a 5.1% comp, 5.8% total revenue growth, an operating margin of 17.4%, and earnings per share growth of 11.7% to $2.19. Every brand and channel delivered a positive comp. Our design advantages and direct sourcing model translate into superior economics, allowing us to increase full-price selling while continuing to give our customers great quality and value. We are making choices that strengthen our brands over time while delivering significant value on a consistent basis. Our 10 great brands and compelling product drive demand, and our integrated operating platform converts that demand into profitable growth,” said Laura Alber, President and Chief Executive Officer.
THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS
•Comparable brand revenue of +5.1%. Net revenue growth of +5.8%.
•Gross margin of 47.6% on a GAAP basis, +150bps to LY driven by (i) IEEPA tariff refunds of +140bps, (ii) occupancy leverage of +40bps, and (iii) higher merchandise margins of +20bps, partially offset by (iv) increased supply chain costs due to higher oil prices of -50bps. Occupancy costs of $210 million, +1.8% to LY.
•Gross margin of 46.2% on a non-GAAP basis, +10bps to LY driven by (i) occupancy leverage of +40bps and (ii) higher merchandise margins of +20bps, partially offset by (iii) increased supply chain costs due to higher oil prices of -50bps. Occupancy costs of $210 million, +1.8% to LY.
•SG&A rate of 28.7% on a GAAP basis, -40bps to LY driven by employment expense leverage. SG&A of $572 million, +4.2% to LY on a GAAP basis.
•SG&A rate of 28.8% on a non-GAAP basis, -30bps to LY driven by employment expense leverage. SG&A of $572 million, +4.3% to LY on a non-GAAP basis.
•Operating income of $377 million with an operating margin of 18.9% on a GAAP basis; or $347 million with an operating margin of 17.4% on a non-GAAP basis. +190bps to LY on a GAAP basis and +40bps to LY on a non-GAAP basis.
•GAAP diluted EPS of $2.38 per share, or $2.19 on a non-GAAP basis. +21.4% to LY on a GAAP basis and +11.7% to LY on a non-GAAP basis.
•Merchandise inventories +7.3% to the third quarter LY to $1.64 billion.
•Maintained strong liquidity position of $992 million in cash and $279 million in operating cash flow, enabling the company to deliver returns to stockholders of $253 million through $163 million in stock repurchases and $90 million in dividends.

TARIFF REFUND
During the third quarter of fiscal 2026, we recognized income from the previously refunded International Emergency Economic Powers Act (“IEEPA”) tariffs, consisting of (i) a reduction of cost of goods sold of $29.3 million related to refunds received for tariffs that had been previously expensed, (ii) related interest income of $0.1 million and (iii) tariff-related adjustments of $0.5 million. Substantially all of our refund claim of $197.8 million has been collected as of November 1, 2026. We have adjusted these items as non-GAAP adjustments. See Exhibit 1 for our GAAP to non-GAAP reconciliation.
OUTLOOK
•We are reiterating our fiscal 2026 guidance.
•In fiscal 2026, we expect annual net revenues in the range of +4.7% to +7.2%, with comps in the range of +4.0% to +6.5%; and an operating margin, on a non-GAAP basis, between 17.8% to 18.2%.
•For fiscal 2026, we expect annual interest income of approximately $30 million and an effective tax rate of approximately 26%, both on a non-GAAP basis.
•Looking beyond fiscal 2026, we remain confident in our growth strategies and operational execution and will provide guidance for fiscal 2027 in March per our usual cadence.

ABOUT WILLIAMS-SONOMA, INC.
Williams-Sonoma, Inc. (NYSE: WSM), a Fortune 500 and S&P 500 company, is the world’s largest digital-first, design-led and sustainable home retailer. The company’s brands — Williams Sonoma, Pottery Barn, Pottery Barn Kids, Pottery Barn Teen, West Elm, Williams Sonoma Home, Rejuvenation, Mark & Graham, GreenRow, and Dormify — represent distinct merchandise strategies that are marketed through e-commerce, direct-mail catalogs, retail stores, and business-to-business. These brands collectively support The Key Rewards, the company's loyalty and credit card program that offers members exclusive benefits. The company operates in the U.S., Puerto Rico, Canada, Australia, and the United Kingdom, and has unaffiliated franchisees that operate stores in Mexico, South Korea, India, and the Philippines.
