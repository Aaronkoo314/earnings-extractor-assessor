OraSure Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results; Strategic Execution Drives Double-Digit Year-Over-Year Growth and Margin Expansion
•14% year-over-year revenue growth in the quarter highlights effectiveness of return-to-growth strategy
•GAAP gross margin improved by over 170 bps vs. prior year to 45.2%
•Received initial orders of Colli-Pee®•Dx and OraQuick® Ebola 2.0 in the quarter
•OTI expects an acceleration of full-year revenue growth in 2027

BETHLEHEM, PA, November 9, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – OraSure Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: OSUR), a leader in point-of-need and home diagnostic tests and sample management solutions, today announced its financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

“Our third quarter results demonstrate the continued execution of our strategy. In addition to delivering double-digit year-over-year revenue growth, we meaningfully expanded gross margins and benefited from operational efficiency initiatives that reduced our cost base,” said Carrie Eglinton Manner, President and CEO of OTI. “We were excited to see the Q3 commercial launches of both our Colli-Pee•Dx Urine Collection Kit and OraQuick Ebola 2.0 Rapid Antigen Test quickly translate into initial orders during the third quarter. These launches marked significant milestones for OTI as we work to further decentralize diagnostics and connect people to care that is more accessible, convenient, affordable, and private.”

“Looking ahead to the fourth quarter and into 2027, we believe we are well positioned for continued revenue growth,” she continued. “Supported by a robust balance sheet, we will continue to invest behind initiatives to accelerate topline growth, improve our operating model, and drive towards profitability.”

•Total net revenues for the third quarter of 2026 of $30.9 million grew 1% sequentially and increased 14% year-over-year.
•Core revenues (all revenues excluding COVID-19 and Risk Assessment Testing revenues) of $30.9 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 14% over the prior year. Diagnostics revenues in the third quarter of 2026 increased 16% year-over-year to $16.8 million, with the increase attributable to higher HIV and HCV revenues and the addition of BioMedomics’ Sickle SCAN® sales. Sample Management Solutions revenues of $12.5 million in the third quarter of 2026 increased 21% year-over-year, with the increase attributable to key commercial customer growth and timing of specific orders.
•GAAP gross margin was 45.2% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 43.5% in the third quarter of 2025. The year-over-year increase was primarily a result of operational efficiencies driving better absorption of fixed overhead costs, partially offset by product mix. Non-GAAP gross margin in the third quarter of 2026 was 45.9% and increased compared to 44.2% in the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 was $13.0 million compared to GAAP operating loss of $16.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP operating loss was $10.2 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to non-GAAP operating loss of $12.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP net loss in the third quarter of 2026 was $13.1 million compared to a GAAP net loss of $13.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Non-GAAP net loss was $10.1 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to non-GAAP net loss of $9.8 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Cash and cash equivalents were $148 million as of September 30, 2026.

Recent Quarterly Developments
•Generated initial sales of our OraQuick Ebola 2.0 Rapid Antigen Test which is authorized for use with whole blood in live patients, as well as cadaveric oral fluid for individuals suspected to have had Ebola disease at the time of death. The test can detect all four Ebola viruses currently known to cause disease in humans: Bundibugyo, Zaire, Sudan, and Taï Forest.
•Generated initial sales of our Colli-Pee•Dx Urine Collection Kit which is cleared for use with Roche’s STI tests for Chlamydia trachomatis (CT), Neisseria gonorrhoeae (NG), Trichomonas vaginalis (TV), and Mycoplasma genitalium (MG). Colli-Pee•Dx enables convenient at-home self-collection of first-void urine by both male and female patients. Specimens can be collected at any time of day in the privacy of the home or any other private setting and are intended for testing on the Roche cobas® molecular diagnostics platform.
•Advanced the early development of HPV testing as a potential future application for Colli-Pee•Dx in collaboration with leading global molecular diagnostics partners, expanding the platform’s potential utility in women’s health and its ability to address unmet needs among under-screened populations.
Financial Guidance
The Company is guiding to Q4 2026 total revenues of $30.0 million to $32.0 million. For the full year 2026, this represents a year-over-year growth rate of 4.7% at the midpoint of the range.
The Company expects to see an acceleration of year-over-year growth in total revenues for the full year 2027 as compared to the full year 2026.

About OraSure Technologies
OraSure Technologies, Inc. (“OraSure” or “OTI”) transforms health through actionable insight and decentralizes diagnostics to connect people to healthcare wherever they are. OraSure improves access, quality, and value of healthcare with innovation in effortless tests and sample management solutions. Together with its wholly-owned subsidiaries, DNA Genotek Inc., Sherlock Biosciences, Inc., and BioMedomics, Inc., OTI is a leader in the development, manufacture, and distribution of rapid diagnostic tests and sample collection and stabilization devices designed to discover and detect critical medical conditions. OraSure’s portfolio of products is sold globally to clinical laboratories, hospitals, physicians’ offices, clinics, public health and community-based organizations, research institutions, government agencies, pharmaceutical companies, and direct to consumers. For more information on OraSure Technologies, please visit www.orasure.com
