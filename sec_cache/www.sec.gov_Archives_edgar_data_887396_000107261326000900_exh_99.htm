EMPIRE PETROLEUM REPORTS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER 2026 AND PROVIDES OPERATIONAL UPDATE

 

TULSA, OK – (NOVEMBER 16, 2026) – Empire Petroleum Corporation (NYSE American: EP) ("Empire" or the "Company"), an oil and gas company with current producing assets in New Mexico, North Dakota, Montana, Texas, and Louisiana, today announced financial results for third-quarter 2026 and an update on current operations.

 

THIRD QUARTER 2026 HIGHLIGHTS

o

Reported third quarter net production volumes of 2,013 barrels of oil equivalent per day (“Boe/d”), including 1,237 barrels of oil per day (“Bbl/d”);

■Boe/d is comprised of 61% oil, 19% natural gas liquids (“NGLs”), and 20% natural gas;

 

oReported third quarter total product revenue of $10.4 million, a net loss of $3.5 million, or ($0.08) per diluted share;
■Adjusted EBITDA of ($0.4) million for third-quarter 2026, compared to $0.1 million in the third quarter of 2025;
■Results for the quarter were primarily influenced by higher realized oil prices from general market pricing trends partially offset by lower production volumes period over period;

 

oEmpire advanced its Texas development program in the Fort Trinidad Field across both the Intermediate Productive Zones (“IPZ”) and Deep Productive Zones (“DPZ”):
■Empire successfully re-entered, underreamed, logged, and cored the Wakefield-Harrison GU B #1 well to 21,006 feet measured depth, approximately 11,000 feet below the field’s historical development depth, recovering a full open-hole log suite and sidewall cores at temperatures reaching 434 degrees Fahrenheit and confirming methane (C1) and C4+ hydrocarbons from approximately 10,000 feet to total depth;
■The re-entry and evaluation were completed for approximately $4.4 million utilizing an existing wellbore, compared with $30.0 to $45.0 million reported by other operators for newly drilled and completed ultra-deep wells in the area;
■Empire re-entered and deepened the Joyce D Unit #102 well to 14,632 feet measured depth in the IPZ;
■Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company commenced re-entry and deepening operations on the Shelly #2 well currently drilling toward a planned total depth of approximately 18,500 feet to test intervals in the IPZ and DPZ, and has recovered whole core from the well, adding to the log and core data set from the Wakefield-Harrison GU B #1 well;
■Empire has observed dozens of hydrocarbon shows, including multiple mud log shows, oil fluorescence and gas to surface, across all three Texas wells, supporting continued evaluation and development planning across the IPZ and DPZ;

 

oThe Company continued its participation in a three-well Louisiana oil and natural gas development program, in which it holds a 25% working interest, with all three wells drilled, logged, and evaluated;
■The three wells remain drilled but uncompleted, with one well deepened to evaluate additional reserve potential, and completion and production planning is ongoing across the program;

oEmpire completed the retrofit and commissioning of its second-generation thermal recovery unit in the northern area of the Starbuck Field as part of its Starbuck Drilling Program (“Starbuck”) in North Dakota;
■The enhanced thermal operations increased production from wells within the associated injection pattern, contributing to an approximately 30% increase in total oil volumes handled through the Starbuck facility compared with the pre-retrofit baseline;
■Empire continued to optimize well completions and execute targeted workover activities across Starbuck, supporting improved well performance and additional production potential, with a third injector expected to be completed in support of further production growth.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

2027 OUTLOOK

“As we look toward 2027, the focus is increasingly on how and where capital is deployed to increase production,” said Phil Mulacek, Chairman of the Board of Empire. “Commodity markets will continue to move through cycles, but the need for reliable North American oil and natural gas remains. In Texas, the work completed this year has significantly expanded our understanding of the Fort Trinidad Field and the development potential across both the IPZ and DPZ. With more than 100 existing wellbores across the field, we believe the technical data we are gathering can help us identify and prioritize the most compelling opportunities as we advance the program. We have had dozens of valid oil shows in the mud logs and gas to surface during drilling operations, while the 2-D, 3-D, and reprocessed seismic data will provide greater technical assurance as we determine where best to complete the wells beginning in the first quarter. The goal of this work is to select the most productive horizontal zones for development. Our focus remains on deploying capital thoughtfully and advancing projects where we believe the combination of resource potential, existing infrastructure, and market conditions can create the greatest shareholder value.”

 

Mike Morrisett, President & CEO, added, “The work completed in 2026 has given us a stronger technical and operational foundation as we enter the next phase of development. We have gathered important data in Texas, demonstrated encouraging performance from our enhanced thermal recovery operations in North Dakota, and continued progressing the Louisiana program toward completion and first production. Our focus in 2027 is to build on that work with disciplined execution, prioritize the opportunities with the greatest potential, and translate our development activity into future growth opportunities.”

 

 

Texas – East Texas Basin & Louisiana

oEmpire plans to continue advancing its Texas development strategy in 2027 through ongoing evaluation, completion, and development activities across the Fort Trinidad Field;
■Technical and operational data obtained through the Company’s control-well program is expected to support future development planning across both the IPZ and DPZ;
■Empire expects to continue utilizing more than 100 existing wellbores and associated infrastructure throughout the Fort Trinidad Field as part of its development strategy;
■Reprocessing of the Company’s existing 3-D seismic, incorporating new log and core data, is underway to further define development targets across the nine potential reservoirs identified on Empire’s acreage;
■The Company is planning new high-resolution 2-D seismic lines focused on the identified zones to help inform future development and completion decisions;

oThe Company expects to advance completion activities in the three-well Louisiana development program in 2027;
■With three wells drilled but uncompleted (“DUCs”), work to advance pipeline optimization is expected to continue;
■The conversion of the DUCs to producing wells is expected to contribute to cash flow and an approximately 20% to 30% increase in production in the first quarter of 2027;
■Additional 3-D seismic and recompletion opportunities are under evaluation, with approximately six wells anticipated in 2027.

North Dakota – Williston Basin

oEmpire is currently working on the conceptual design and budgeting of an additional second-generation thermal recovery unit planned for the southern area of the Starbuck Field, with fabrication and installation expected to follow completion of this phase;
■The planned project is designed around a one-injector, two-producer (1:2) thermal injection pattern and is intended to further expand thermal recovery operations across the Starbuck Field;
■The project also includes associated surface facility upgrades and improvements to the Starbuck Field’s water supply infrastructure, with the objective of providing higher-quality feedwater for steam generation, improving thermal-unit reliability, and enhancing long-term operating performance;
■With the third Starbuck injector expected to be completed, the Company anticipates an approximately 20% to 30% increase in North Dakota production in the first quarter of 2027.

 

New Mexico – Permian Basin

oEmpire continues to work with the State of New Mexico and the New Mexico Oil Conservation Commission regarding the Company’s rights to the Residual Oil Zone in the Eunice Monument South Unit’s Unitized Interval;
oThe Company expects final resolution of this matter to result in a meaningful reduction in operating expenses and contribute to improved financial performance in New Mexico going forward.

Net sales volumes for third-quarter 2026 were 2,013 Boe/d, including 1,237 barrels of oil per day; 376 barrels of NGLs per day, and 2,404 thousand cubic feet per day (“Mcf/d”) or 401 Boe/d of natural gas. Oil sales volumes for third-quarter 2026 decreased compared to third-quarter 2025 primarily due to natural decline and certain wells within North Dakota that were down for Empire’s steam unit performance enhancement projects which began in the second quarter of 2026.

 

Empire reported third-quarter 2026 total product revenue of $10.4 million versus $9.4 million in third-quarter 2025. The increase is primarily due to higher realized oil prices from general market pricing trends partially offset by lower production volumes period over period.

 

Realized oil prices for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $76.06 per barrel, while realized prices for the same period in the prior year were $61.00 per barrel, an increase of approximately 25% including the impact of the Company’s hedging contracts. The average oil price per barrel excluding the effect of net settlements received (paid) for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $83.07. There were no impacts to prior period pricing due to no open hedging contracts during the period.

 

Lease operating expenses in third-quarter 2026 increased to $6.5 million versus $5.7 million for third-quarter 2025, primarily due to a higher workover expense partially offset by lower overall production and efforts by the Company to reduce overall operating costs. The increase in workover expense period over period is primarily due to a reclass of certain capitalized costs in Texas for projects which were reevaluated. Workover expenses were approximately $1.9 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $0.4 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

Production and ad valorem taxes for third-quarter 2026 were $0.9 million versus $0.8 million in third-quarter 2025, as a result of higher product revenues.

 

Depreciation, Depletion, and Amortization (“DD&A”) and Accretion for third-quarter 2026 was $2.3 million versus $3.3 million for third-quarter 2025. The decrease in DD&A is primarily due to the impact of impairments in fourth-quarter 2025 and lower production volumes period over period, partially offset by the additional interests acquired in New Mexico in the first quarter of 2026 and certain interests in North Dakota finalized in second-quarter 2026. Accretion increased slightly from prior period as well due to these same events.

 

General and administrative expenses, excluding share-based compensation expense, were $2.8 million, or $15.26 per Boe in third-quarter 2026 versus $2.9 million, or $13.06 per Boe in third-quarter 2025. The slight decrease in expenses was primarily due to a decrease in employee costs due to lower headcount in 2026.

 

Total interest expense for the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 resulted in a slight decrease due to a lower average outstanding balance and interest rate under the Company’s credit facility offset by additional notes related to equipment and vehicles.

 

Empire recorded a net loss of $3.5 million in third-quarter 2026, or ($0.08) per diluted share, versus a third-quarter 2025 net loss of $3.8 million, or ($0.11) per diluted share.

 

Adjusted EBITDA was ($0.4) million for third-quarter 2026 compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $0.1 million in third-quarter 2025. 

 

 

 

 

CAPITAL SPENDING, BALANCE SHEET & LIQUIDITY

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Empire incurred approximately $14.0 million of total additions to oil and natural gas properties, which is primarily from the Company’s gas development program in Texas.

 

As of September 30, 2026, Empire had approximately $2.6 million in cash on hand, and approximately $1.2 million available on its credit facility.

 

Empire issued a convertible promissory note and received proceeds in the aggregate principal amount of approximately $3.3 million to Petroleum Independent & Exploration, LLC, with a maturity date of March 10, 2028.

CAUTIONARY NOTE REGARDING WELL DATA

Log data, sidewall core recovery and hydrocarbon shows indicate the presence of hydrocarbons and provide information on reservoir quality. They are not a measurement of producible reserves and are not indicative of commercial flow rates. The Wakefield-Harrison GU B #1 has not been completed or production tested, and no flow rate, reserve or resource estimate is being reported for this well. Results reported by other operators are from wells in different locations, in different reservoir intervals, with different completion designs, and are not a prediction of results Empire may achieve.
