AXIS CAPITAL REPORTS THIRD QUARTER NET INCOME AVAILABLE TO COMMON SHAREHOLDERS OF $202 MILLION, or $2.75 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE AND OPERATING INCOME OF $229 MILLION, or $3.12 PER DILUTED COMMON SHARE

For the third quarter of 2026, the Company reports:
•Annualized return on average common equity ("ROACE") of 13.8% and annualized operating ROACE of 15.7%
•Combined ratio of 94.0% and underwriting income of $134 million
•Book value per diluted common share of $79.06

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reports:
•Net income available to common shareholders of $699 million, or $9.42 per diluted common share and operating income of $697 million, or $9.39 per diluted common share
•Annualized ROACE and annualized operating ROACE of 16.1%
•Combined ratio of 92.3% and underwriting income of $464 million
•Book value per diluted common share, excluding accumulated other comprehensive income (loss) ("AOCI") of $84.33, an increase of $10.64, or 14.4%, compared to $73.69 at September 30, 2025

Pembroke, Bermuda, October 28, 2026 - AXIS Capital Holdings Limited ("AXIS Capital" or "AXIS" or "the Company") (NYSE: AXS) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Commenting on the third quarter 2026 financial results, Vince Tizzio, President and CEO of AXIS Capital said:

"This was a strong quarter for AXIS, as our team leaned into attractive underwriting opportunities while practicing disciplined cycle management in a transitioning market. Our performance included a 15.7% annualized operating return-on-equity, $3.12 in operating earnings-per-share, and gross premiums rose 11%. Over the past twelve months, AXIS has generated a 14.4% increase in diluted book value per share, ex AOCI.

"Our Insurance business produced a 13% increase in gross premiums with a 92.0% combined ratio and an 87.2% current accident year ex-cat combined ratio. Leveraging our global platform, we drove diversified premium volume with meaningful contributions from our expanded classes and AXIS Capacity Solutions capability. Within AXIS Re, we generated our 11th consecutive profitable quarter.

"As we look ahead to the remainder of this year and to 2027, AXIS is well-positioned. The strength of our balance sheet provides us with the flexibility to make smart investments in our business spanning products, platforms, and talent. We are excited about where AXIS stands today, supported by a clear strategy, balanced and resilient portfolio, enhanced operating model, and deep and capable team."

Third Quarter and Year-to-date Consolidated Results*(1)

•Net income available to common shareholders for the third quarter of 2026 was $202 million, a decrease of $92 million, or 32%, compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Net income available to common shareholders year-to-date was $699 million, an increase of $3 million, or 0.4%, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
•Operating income(2) for the third quarter of 2026 was $229 million, a decrease of $26 million, or 10%, compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Operating income year-to-date was $697 million, a decrease of $78 million, or 10%, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
•Underwriting income(3) for the third quarter of 2026 was $134 million, a decrease of $55 million, or 29%, compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Underwriting income year-to-date was $464 million, a decrease of $77 million, or 14%, compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
•Fees related to arrangements with strategic capital partners for the third quarter of 2026 were $23 million, compared to $19 million for the third quarter of 2025. Fees year-to-date were $67 million, compared to $55 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
•Book yield of fixed maturities was 4.9% at September 30, 2026, compared to 4.6% at September 30, 2025. The market yield was 5.8% at September 30, 2026.
•Reorganization expenses for the third quarter were $nil and year-to-date were $29 million, primarily related to initiatives undertaken to streamline our operations, including actions taken to optimize our reinsurance platform and costs associated with transitions in executive leadership.
•Total capital returned to common shareholders was $141 million, including common share repurchases of $109 million pursuant to our Board-authorized share repurchase program, and common share dividends of $32 million in the quarter.
•Book value per diluted common share was $79.06 at September 30, 2026, a decrease of $1.61, or 2.0%, compared to June 30, 2026.
•Book value per diluted common share increased by $5.24, or 7.1%, over the past twelve months, driven by net income, partially offset by common share repurchases, common share dividends of $1.76 per share and net unrealized investment losses.
•Book value per diluted common share, excluding AOCI(4) was $84.33 at September 30, 2026, an increase of $2.20, or 2.7%, compared to $82.13 at June 30, 2026, and an increase of $10.64, or 14.4%, compared to $73.69 at September 30, 2025.

Three months ended September 30, 2026
•Gross premiums written increased by $234 million, or 11%, to $2.4 billion with an increase of $217 million, or 13% in the insurance segment, and an increase of $17 million, or 4% in the reinsurance segment.
•Net premiums written increased by $132 million, or 10%, to $1.5 billion with an increase of $144 million, or 13% in the insurance segment, partially offset by a decrease of $12 million, or 5% in the reinsurance segment.
•Pre-tax, catastrophe and weather-related losses, net of reinsurance, were $75 million ($57 million after-tax) (Insurance: $75 million; Reinsurance: $nil), or 4.6 points, including $44 million or 2.7 points attributable to the Middle East conflict and the Russia-Ukraine war. The remaining losses of $31 million or 1.9 points were attributable to weather-related events.
•Net favorable prior year reserve development was $15 million (Insurance: $12 million; Reinsurance: $3 million), compared to $19 million in 2025.
•The acquisition cost ratio increased by 2.8 points primarily related to new property and professional lines business associated with our AXIS Capacity Solutions ("ACS") capability, and a greater mix of short-tail lines, including marine war business, which have relatively higher acquisition cost ratios.

Nine months ended September 30, 2026
•Gross premiums written increased by $689 million, or 9% ($621 million, or 8%, on a constant currency basis(7)), to $8.1 billion with an increase of $841 million, or 16% in the insurance segment, partially offset by a decrease of $151 million, or 7% in the reinsurance segment.
•Net premiums written increased by $259 million, or 5% ($195 million, or 4%, on a constant currency basis), to $5.0 billion with an increase of $473 million, or 14% in the insurance segment, partially offset by a decrease of $214 million, or 16% in the reinsurance segment.
•Pre-tax, catastrophe and weather-related losses, net of reinsurance, were $203 million ($159 million after-tax) (Insurance: $200 million; Reinsurance: $3 million), or 4.4 points, including natural catastrophe losses of $112 million or 2.4 points, primarily attributable to U.S. winter storms and other weather-related events. The remaining losses of $90 million or 2.0 points were attributable to the Middle East conflict and the Russia-Ukraine war.
•Net favorable prior year reserve development was $48 million (Insurance: $39 million; Reinsurance: $9 million), compared to $57 million in 2025.

Rationale for the Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

We present our results of operations in a way we believe will be meaningful and useful to investors, analysts, rating agencies and others who use our financial information to evaluate our performance. Some of the measurements we use are considered non-GAAP financial measures under SEC rules and regulations. In this press release, we present underwriting-related general and administrative expenses, consolidated underwriting income (loss), current accident year loss ratio, catastrophe and weather-related losses ratio, current accident year loss ratio, excluding catastrophe and weather-related losses, current accident year combined ratio, current accident year combined ratio, excluding catastrophe and weather-related losses, operating income (loss) (in total and on a per share basis), annualized operating return on average common equity ("operating ROACE"), amounts presented on a constant currency basis, pre-tax, total return on average cash and investments excluding foreign exchange movements, and book value per diluted common share, excluding AOCI which are non-GAAP financial measures as defined in SEC Regulation G. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures, which may be defined and calculated differently by other companies, help explain and enhance the understanding of our results of operations. However, these measures should not be viewed as a substitute for those determined in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America ("U.S. GAAP").

Underwriting-Related General and Administrative Expenses
Underwriting-related general and administrative expenses include those general and administrative expenses that are incremental and/or directly attributable to our underwriting operations. While this measure is presented in the 'Segment Information' note to our Consolidated Financial Statements, it is considered a non-GAAP financial measure when presented elsewhere on a consolidated basis.

Corporate expenses include holding company costs necessary to support our worldwide insurance and reinsurance operations and costs associated with operating as a publicly-traded company. As these costs are not incremental and/or directly attributable to our underwriting operations, these costs are excluded from underwriting-related general and administrative expenses, and therefore, consolidated underwriting income (loss). General and administrative expenses, the most comparable GAAP financial measure to underwriting-related general and administrative expenses, also includes corporate expenses.

The reconciliation of consolidated underwriting-related general and administrative expenses to general and administrative expenses, the most comparable GAAP financial measure, is presented in the 'Consolidated Segmental Data' section of this press release.

Consolidated Underwriting Income (Loss)
Consolidated underwriting income (loss) is a pre-tax measure of underwriting profitability that takes into account net premiums earned and other insurance related income (loss) as revenues and net losses and loss expenses, acquisition costs and underwriting-related general and administrative expenses as expenses. While this measure is presented in the 'Segment Information' note to our Consolidated Financial Statements, it is considered a non-GAAP financial measure when presented elsewhere on a consolidated basis.

We evaluate our underwriting results separately from the performance of our investment portfolio. As a result, we believe it is appropriate to exclude net investment income and net investment gains (losses) from our underwriting profitability measure.

Foreign exchange losses (gains) in our consolidated statements of operations primarily relate to the impact of foreign exchange rate movements on our net insurance-related liabilities. However, we manage our investment portfolio in such a way that unrealized and realized foreign exchange losses (gains) on our investment portfolio, including unrealized foreign exchange losses (gains) on our equity securities, and foreign exchange losses (gains) realized on the sale of available for sale investments and equity securities recognized in net investment gains (losses), and unrealized foreign exchange losses (gains) on our available for sale investments in other comprehensive income (loss), generally offset a large portion of the foreign exchange losses (gains) arising from our underwriting portfolio, thereby minimizing the impact of foreign exchange rate movements on total shareholders' equity.
