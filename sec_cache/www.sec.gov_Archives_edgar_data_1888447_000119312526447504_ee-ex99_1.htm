Excelerate Energy Reports Strong Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

The Woodlands, TX, November 4, 2026 – Excelerate Energy, Inc. (NYSE: EE) (Excelerate or the Company) today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

RECENT Highlights

•
Reported Net Income of $60.0 million for the third quarter
•
Reported Adjusted EBITDA of $133.5 million for the third quarter
•
Executed an 11-year LNG supply agreement with AxDo Bahamas Ltd. ("AxDo") supporting the development of the Bahamas LNG Hub, with AxDo serving as anchor customer of Excelerate's planned offshore LNG storage and distribution terminal near the island of Grand Bahama
•
Executed an agreement with Petróleo Brasileiro S.A. ("Petrobras") to extend the FSRU Sequoia contract by five years through 2038 and to install a reliquefaction unit onboard the vessel during its next dry dock in 2028
•
Declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.09 per share, payable on December 3, 2026

 

CEO COmment

"Excelerate delivered record quarterly earnings, reflecting the essential role our infrastructure plays in strengthening energy security for the markets we serve. During the quarter, our teams continued to operate safely and reliably for our customers, and I'm proud of what they accomplished," said Steven Kobos, President and CEO of Excelerate.

Kobos continued, "We also made meaningful commercial progress, securing new long-term agreements, extending existing ones, and expanding in the Caribbean with the planned Bahamas LNG Hub. The opportunities we've advanced span our footprint, adding diversity to our portfolio and reinforcing our position as a global provider of critical LNG infrastructure. This progress expands our contracted earnings base and advances our sequenced growth strategy, which we believe will create lasting value for our shareholders."

Key Commercial Updates

Excelerate has executed an 11-year LNG supply agreement with AxDo, a joint venture between Axpo, an international leader in energy trading and marketing, and Bahamas-based energy and maritime logistics company Domicisle LNG, supporting development of the Bahamas LNG Hub. Under the agreement, AxDo, established in October 2026 to conduct small-scale LNG and bunkering operations in

 

 

the region, will serve as an anchor customer of the Company's planned offshore LNG storage and distribution terminal near the island of Grand Bahama. The supply agreement provides for LNG deliveries of up to 16 TBtu per year, with additional capacity available to support further sales to AxDo and other customers throughout the region. The Bahamas LNG Hub is designed to provide LNG storage and distribution services for marine bunkering, power generation, and other downstream customers in The Bahamas and throughout the Caribbean. Initial LNG deliveries are expected to begin in the first quarter of 2027, utilizing the existing FSRU, LNG supply, and logistics capabilities of the Company's Jamaica platform. A second phase is expected to include deployment of a Floating Storage Unit ("FSU") near the island of Grand Bahama in the second half of 2028, subject to final permitting and construction timelines, and other contractual provisions. The FSU will serve as the offshore LNG storage and distribution terminal.

 

In October 2025, Excelerate executed a definitive commercial agreement with a subsidiary of Iraq’s Ministry of Electricity for the development of the country’s first LNG import terminal. The integrated project includes a five-year agreement for regasification services and LNG supply with a customer extension option, and a minimum contracted offtake of 250 million standard cubic feet per day. Critical materials needed for jetty reinforcement and construction of the fixed terminal infrastructure are arriving in country. In addition, the construction workforce for the terminal is on site, and the Company expects activity to ramp up over the coming weeks as work on the jetty and terminal facilities accelerates. In response to the ongoing conflict in the Arabian Gulf, Excelerate expects to utilize an FSRU already positioned in the region to serve the terminal, eliminating the need for an FSRU to transit the Strait of Hormuz. The Company is also advancing LNG supply arrangements, including potential supply from intra-basin sources. Excelerate continues to prioritize the safety and security of its people and operations as it advances the project toward its targeted second-quarter 2027 startup.

 

In June 2026, Excelerate executed a long-term agreement with Sociedad Portuaria Puerto Bahia, a subsidiary of Frontera Energy Corporation, to provide regasification services to a new LNG import terminal under development in Colombia. The agreement has a seven-year term with multiple extension options and secures long-term utilization of the Company's assets in a market working to strengthen energy security and diversify its sources of natural gas supply. The agreement contemplates the redeployment of the FSRU Express. However, in response to the ongoing conflict in the Arabian Gulf, Excelerate expects to utilize other FSRUs in the Company's portfolio located outside the Gulf to serve the terminal in the near term. This approach eliminates the need for an FSRU to transit the Strait of Hormuz to support the Colombia deployment. Service under the agreement is expected to commence in the first quarter of 2027.

 

In August 2026, Excelerate signed an agreement with Petrobras for a five-year extension of the FSRU Sequoia contract beyond its current term, extending the Company's relationship with Petrobras through 2038. As part of the agreement, Excelerate will invest in the installation of a reliquefaction unit on the Sequoia during its next scheduled dry dock in 2028. Once installed, the technology is expected to eliminate excess LNG losses due to boil-off, reduce Excelerate's Scope 1 emissions, and improve the terminal's operating performance and useful life. The agreement also provides for a modest increase in contracted revenue beginning in 2028, generating incremental earnings ahead of the extension period.

 

In July 2026, Excelerate executed an agreement to acquire an LNG carrier, the Methane Patricia Camila, which is expected to serve as the dedicated vessel for our first FSRU conversion project. In October 2026, we awarded Seatrium an engineering, procurement, construction, installation and commissioning contract for the conversion. The converted FSRU is expected to be available for commercial deployment in 2028.

 

Liquidity and capital resources

As of September 30, 2026, Excelerate had $356.7 million in unrestricted cash and cash equivalents and the Company had no letters of credit under its revolving credit facility. All of the $500 million of capacity under the revolving credit facility was available for borrowings as of September 30, 2026.

The dividend is payable on December 3, 2026, to Class A common stockholders of record as of the close of business on November 18, 2026.

ABOUT EXCELERATE ENERGY

Excelerate Energy, Inc. is a U.S.-based LNG and power infrastructure company located in The Woodlands, Texas. Excelerate helps countries around the world enhance their energy security by providing reliable energy infrastructure and increasing access to global LNG markets. The Company delivers services along the LNG to power value chain, including floating regasification terminals, downstream infrastructure development, LNG supply, and power generation. Excelerate has a presence in Abu Dhabi, Antwerp, Aqaba, Boston, Buenos Aires, Chattogram, Dhaka, Doha, Dubai, Hanoi, Helsinki, Jamaica, Karachi, London, Rio de Janeiro, Singapore, Washington, D.C., and Wilhelmshaven. For more information, please visit www.excelerateenergy.com.

USE OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

The Company reports financial results in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). Included in this press release are certain financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with GAAP. They are designed to supplement, and not substitute, Excelerate’s financial information presented in accordance with GAAP. The non-GAAP measures as defined by Excelerate may not be comparable to similar non-GAAP measures presented by other companies, and you are cautioned not to place undue reliance on this information. The presentation of such measures, which may include adjustments to exclude non-recurring items, should not be construed as an inference that Excelerate’s future results, cash flows or leverage will be unaffected by other non-recurring items. Management believes that the following non-GAAP financial measures provide investors with additional useful information in evaluating the Company's performance and valuation. See the reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the most comparable GAAP financial measure, including those measures presented as part of the Company’s 2026 Financial Outlook, in the section titled “Non-GAAP Reconciliation” below.

Adjusted Gross Margin

The Company uses Adjusted Gross Margin, a non-GAAP financial measure, which it defines as revenues less cost of LNG, gas and power and operating expenses, excluding depreciation and amortization, to measure its operational financial performance. Management believes Adjusted Gross Margin is useful because it provides insight into profitability and true operating performance excluding the implications of the historical cost basis of the Company’s assets.

Adjusted Net Income

The Company uses Adjusted Net Income, a non-GAAP financial measure, which it defines as net income plus tax-effected transition and transaction expenses. Management believes Adjusted Net Income is useful because it provides insight into profitability excluding the impact of non-recurring charges related to the Jamaica acquisition.

Adjusted EBITDA

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure included as a supplemental disclosure because management believes it is a useful indicator of the Company’s operating performance. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income before interest expense, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, accretion, non-cash long-term incentive compensation expense and items such as charges and non-recurring expenses that management does not consider as part of assessing ongoing operating performance.

The Company adjusts net income for the items listed above to arrive at Adjusted EBITDA because these amounts can vary substantially from company to company within its industry depending upon accounting methods and book values of assets, capital structures and the method by which the assets were acquired. Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered as an alternative to, or more meaningful than, net income as determined in accordance with GAAP or as an indicator of the Company’s operating performance or liquidity. This measure has limitations as certain excluded items are significant components in understanding and assessing a company’s financial performance, such as a company’s cost of capital and tax structure, as well as the historic costs of depreciable assets, none of which

3

 

are components of Adjusted EBITDA. The Company's presentation of Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that its results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. For the foregoing reasons, Adjusted EBITDA has significant limitations that affect its use as an indicator of the Company’s profitability and valuation.

Adjusted Earnings Per Share

The Company uses Adjusted Earnings Per Share ("EPS"), a non-GAAP financial measure, which it defines as diluted EPS plus the per share impact of its tax-effected transition and transaction expenses. Management believes Adjusted EPS is useful because it provides insight on per share profitability excluding the impact of non-recurring charges related to the Jamaica acquisition.
