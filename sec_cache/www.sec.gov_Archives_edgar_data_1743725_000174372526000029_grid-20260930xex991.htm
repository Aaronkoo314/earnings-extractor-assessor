Grid Dynamics Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Revenues of $114.3 million and Record AI Revenues of 31%
San Ramon, Calif. — October 29, 2026 – Grid Dynamics Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: GDYN) (“Grid Dynamics” or the “Company”), a leader in enterprise-level AI and digital transformation, today announced results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Revenues Performance
We are pleased to report third quarter 2026 revenues of $114.3 million, above the high end of our guidance range of $112.0 million to $114.0 million that we provided in July 2026.
Our Technology, Media and Telecom (“TMT”) vertical was our principal growth driver, representing 32.3% of the third quarter revenues. TMT revenues increased 29.3% year-over-year and 7.6% sequentially, driven by higher demand from our largest technology customers as well as new customers. Retail remained our second-largest vertical, contributing 27.2% of total revenues for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 13.3% year-over-year and 8.5% sequentially, driven by increased demand from specialty retailers. The Finance vertical contributed 22.5% of the third quarter revenues, an increase of 3.9% sequentially, supported by ongoing demand from global capital market customers. Our Consumer Packaged Goods (“CPG”) and Manufacturing vertical represented 10.9% of quarterly revenues and increased 5.8% sequentially. Lastly, the Healthcare and Pharma, and Other verticals contributed 1.8% and 5.3% of the third quarter revenues, respectively.
“Q3 2026 was a pivotal quarter for Grid Dynamics, delivering record revenue, record EBITDA, and AI-native programs in production at some of the world’s largest enterprises. AI revenue has grown over 45% year-over-year for three consecutive quarters and is now 31% of total revenue. We delivered a 40% gross margin and a 20% EBITDA margin, exceeding our 300 basis point margin commitment a quarter sooner than planned. This is a reflection of substantial enhancements to our business model, not a one-time event.

We are growing faster, more profitably, and precisely where enterprise AI spend is accelerating. We enter the fourth quarter with confidence and a positive backdrop for 2027, backed by the momentum and structural foundation to sustain it in the years ahead,” said Leonard Livschitz, Chief Executive Officer.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Total revenues were $114.3 million, up 5.7% sequentially and 9.7% year-over-year.
•GAAP gross profit was $46.1 million, or 40.3% of revenues, compared to $34.7 million, or 33.3% of revenues, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Non-GAAP gross profit was $46.5 million, or 40.6% of revenues, compared to $35.2 million, or 33.8% of revenues, in the third quarter of 2025.
•GAAP net income was $6.2 million, or $0.08 per share, based on 81.9 million diluted weighted-average common shares outstanding in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.2 million, or $0.01 per share, based on 85.8 million diluted weighted-average common shares outstanding, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Non-GAAP net income was $14.7 million, or $0.18 per diluted share, based on 81.9 million diluted weighted-average common shares outstanding in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $8.2 million, or $0.09 per diluted share, based on 85.8 million diluted weighted-average common shares outstanding, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Non-GAAP EBITDA (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization, other income and expenses, fair value adjustments, stock-based compensation, transaction and transformation-related costs, and restructuring costs as well as geographic reorganization expenses), a non-GAAP metric, was $22.9 million, or 20.0% of revenues, compared to $12.7 million, or 12.2% of revenues, in the third quarter of 2025.
See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” and “Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Information” below for a discussion of our non-GAAP measures.


Cash Flow and Other Metrics
•Cash provided by operating activities was $30.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $30.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
•The Company spent $35.5 million to repurchase 5.3 million shares during the nine months ended September 30, 2026, under its $50 million share repurchase program, which included $6.7 million, or 0.9 million shares, during the third quarter of 2026.
•Cash and cash equivalents totaled $304.3 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $342.1 million as of December 31, 2025.
•Total headcount was 4,559 as of September 30, 2026, compared with 4,971 as of September 30, 2025.

Financial Outlook

Fourth Quarter
•The Company expects revenues in the fourth quarter of 2026 to be in the range of $113.5 to $115.5 million.
•Non-GAAP EBITDA in the fourth quarter of 2026 is expected to be between $20.0 and $22.0 million.
•For the fourth quarter of 2026, we expect our basic share count to be in the 81 - 82 million range and diluted share count to be in the 82 - 83 million range.
Grid Dynamics is not able, at this time, to provide GAAP targets for net income/(loss) for the fourth quarter of 2026 because of the difficulty of estimating certain items excluded from Non-GAAP EBITDA that cannot be reasonably predicted, such as interest income, taxes, other income/(expenses), fair-value adjustments, geographic reorganization expenses, restructuring expenses, transaction-related costs and charges related to stock-based compensation expense. The effect of these excluded items may be significant.
