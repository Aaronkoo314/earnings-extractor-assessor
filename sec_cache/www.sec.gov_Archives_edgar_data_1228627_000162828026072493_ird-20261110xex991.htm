Opus Genetics Announces Financial Results for
Third Quarter 2026 and Provides Corporate Update
OPGx-BEST1 Cohort 2 Dosing Complete and Data Expected in Q1 2027
OPGx-LCA5 Registrational Phase 3 Trial Fully Enrolled with Initiation of Dosing Expected in Q4 2026
FDA Clearance of IND for OPGx-RDH12, with Phase 1/2 Trial Dosing Planned for Q4 2026
FDA Approval of Phentolamine Ophthalmic Solution 0.75% for Presbyopia Triggers Milestone Payment
Cash Runway into 2029 Expected to Support Multiple Clinical Inflection Points and
Opportunities for Priority Review Vouchers
RESEARCH TRIANGLE PARK, N.C. – November 10, 2026 - Opus Genetics, Inc. (Nasdaq: IRD) (the “Company” or “Opus Genetics”), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing gene therapies to restore vision and prevent blindness in patients with inherited retinal diseases (IRDs), today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.
“The last several months have marked a period of tremendous advancement for our leading gene therapy programs,” said George Magrath, M.D., Chief Executive Officer of Opus Genetics. “Positive Cohort 1 data from our Phase 1/2 trial of OPGx-BEST1 provide important evidence of its potential to improve both visual function and retinal structure in patients with BEST1-related retinal disease. With U.S. Food & Drug Administration (FDA) alignment on a potential pivotal endpoint and Cohort 2 over-enrolled with 11 participants, we expect to have data in Q1 2027, followed by dosing in a potential pivotal Phase 3 trial anticipated to begin in the second half of 2027. For OPGx-LCA5, we have fully enrolled our registrational Phase 3 trial and expect to begin dosing participants this quarter. We are also advancing our third gene therapy program into the clinic following IND clearance for OPGx-RDH12.”
“The FDA approval of phentolamine ophthalmic solution 0.75% for presbyopia marks the second FDA-approved indication for RYZUMVI®, demonstrating the capabilities of our clinical and regulatory teams. With our cash runway into 2029, we remain focused on bringing potentially transformative gene therapies to patients with rare inherited retinal diseases,” concluded Dr. Magrath.
Pipeline Updates
OPGx-BEST1
•Announced positive 3- and 6-month results from the low-dose Cohort 1 of the Phase 1/2 BIRD-1 trial, with a favorable safety profile and no serious adverse events or dose-limiting toxicities.
•All five participants demonstrated clinically meaningful improvement in visual function, including retinal sensitivity improvement by microperimetry in 75% of evaluable participants (3/4), and four of five showed structural improvements.
•Aligned with the FDA on a potential pivotal endpoint of ≥3 dB microperimetry improvement in ≥5 prespecified loci, in conjunction with a patient-reported outcome.
•Completed enrollment and dosing of 11 participants in the high-dose Cohort 2, with topline data, including 3-month data from Cohort 2 and 6-month data for the three Cohort 1 participants with Best vitelliform macular dystrophy, expected in Q1 2027.
OPGx-LCA5
•The registrational Phase 3 trial is fully enrolled with 8 participants, and initiation of dosing is expected in Q4 2026.
•Phase 1/2 data presented at Retina in Rio 2026 showed sustained improvements in visual acuity and light sensitivity in adults through 24 months and encouraging results in pediatric participants through six months.
OPGx-RDH12
•Received FDA clearance of the Investigational New Drug (IND) application for OPGx-RDH12 for RDH12-associated Leber congenital amaurosis (RDH12-LCA), with initiation of dosing in a Phase 1/2 trial expected in Q4 2026.


Phentolamine Ophthalmic Solution 0.75%
•Received FDA approval of the supplemental New Drug Application (sNDA) for phentolamine ophthalmic solution 0.75% (RYZUMVI®) for the treatment of presbyopia in adults, triggering a regulatory milestone payment to Opus Genetics from commercial partner, Viatris Inc.
Recent Medical Presentations
Opus Genetics delivered numerous data presentations at prominent medical meetings, including the Euretina Innovation Spotlight, Euretina 2026, and Retina in Rio 2026 where OPGx-BEST1 Cohort 1 data and OPGx-LCA5 Phase 1/2 data were presented. All materials can be found on the Publications & Presentations page of the Company website.
Management Team
Opus Genetics recently added Andrew Murphy, Ph.D., as Chief Scientific Officer, and Jamie Lynne Hart, M.S., M.P.H., as Executive Director, Executive Operations, strengthening its scientific and operational leadership.
Financial Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026
Cash Position: As of September 30, 2026, Opus Genetics had cash and cash equivalents of $89.0 million. With the current cash and cash equivalents, and potential future funding under the note purchase agreement with Oberland Capital Management, the Company believes its aggregate cash resources will fund operations into 2029.
Revenue: License and collaborations revenue totaled $1.1 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $3.1 million for the same period in 2025. Revenue in both periods came primarily from reimbursement of research and development (R&D) services based on the Company’s collaboration with Viatris, Inc.
Research and Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $14.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $6.4 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to higher manufacturing and clinical costs associated with the Company's IRD programs, partially offset by lower clinical costs associated with the Phentolamine Ophthalmic Solution 0.75% programs. Research and development expenses included $0.6 million and $0.2 million in stock-based compensation expense for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
General and Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $5.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $5.0 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to higher employee compensation costs on a period-over-period basis. General and administrative expenses included $1.0 million and $0.6 million in stock-based compensation expense for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
Net Loss: Net loss for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $46.5 million, or ($0.46) per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $17.5 million, or ($0.25) per basic and diluted share, for the same period in 2025. The increase in net loss was primarily due to higher expense period over period of $19.1 million from the fair value change in instruments measured at fair value, increased R&D expense period over period of $8.1 million, and lower license and collaborations revenue of $2.0 million period over period.
About Opus Genetics
Opus Genetics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing gene therapies to restore vision and prevent blindness in patients with inherited retinal diseases (IRDs). The Company is developing one-time treatments designed to address the underlying genetic causes of severe retinal disorders. The Company’s pipeline includes seven AAV-based programs, led by OPGx-LCA5 for LCA5-related mutations and OPGx-BEST1 for BEST1-related retinal degeneration, with OPGx-RDH12 cleared to enter the clinic, and additional candidates targeting MERTK, RHO, CNGB1 and NMNAT1. The Company is based in Research Triangle Park, NC. For more information, visit www.opusgtx.com.
