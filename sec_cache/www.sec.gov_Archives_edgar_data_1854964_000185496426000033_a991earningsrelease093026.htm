NewLake Capital Partners Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Third Quarter 2026 Revenue Totaled $12.0 Million

Third Quarter 2026 Net Income Attributable to Common Stockholders Totaled $4.2 Million, Funds from Operations Totaled $10.0 Million, and Adjusted Funds from Operations Totaled $10.3 Million

Conference Call and Webcast Scheduled for November 5, 2026, at 11 a.m. Eastern Time

New Canaan, CT, November 4, 2026 — NewLake Capital Partners, Inc. (OCTQX: NLCP) (the “Company” or “NewLake”), a leading provider of real estate capital to state-licensed cannabis operators, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“Our third quarter financial results were in line with our expectations, and year to date we collected 99.7% of contractual rent. Notably, we strengthened our balance sheet by repaying $7.6 million of debt, ending the quarter with no debt outstanding and $17.9 million in cash,” said Anthony Coniglio, NewLake’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “During the third quarter we acquired a dispensary property in Kentucky, and subsequent to quarter end we acquired dispensary properties in Michigan and Kentucky. With a disciplined focus on property-level cash flow, a debt-free balance sheet and available credit capacity, we believe NewLake is well positioned to pursue growth opportunities as the cannabis regulatory landscape matures.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

◦Total revenue of $12.0 million.
◦Net income attributable to common stockholders totaled $4.2 million, or $0.20 per share of common stock.
◦Funds From Operations (“FFO”)(1) totaled $10.0 million, or $0.47 per share of common stock.
◦Adjusted Funds From Operations (“AFFO”)(1) totaled $10.3 million, or $0.49 per share of common stock.
◦Declared a third quarter dividend of $0.43 per share of common stock, equivalent to an annualized dividend of $1.72 per common share.

Balance Sheet and Third Quarter Highlights as of September 30, 2026

◦Cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, were $17.9 million.
◦Gross real estate assets of $433.8 million, including one property classified as Real Estate Held for Sale.
◦On August 4, 2026, the Company purchased a dispensary located in Kentucky for approximately $0.6 million and committed to fund approximately $1.6 million for improvements.
◦No outstanding debt.

Recent Developments

◦Subsequent to quarter end, the Company acquired two dispensary properties located in Michigan and Kentucky for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $1.3 million and committed to fund approximately $1.7 million in property improvements. The properties were leased to an affiliate of an existing tenant.
◦In November 2026, the Company's Board of Directors authorized an extension of the expiration date of the Company's stock repurchase program to December 31, 2028. Approximately $8.2 million remains available for future repurchases under the program.







(1) FFO and AFFO are presented on a dilutive basis.

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company generated total revenue of approximately $12.0 million, representing a 4.5% decrease compared to $12.6 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease was driven by the loss of rental income from three properties that became available for lease during 2025. The decrease was partially offset by annual contractual rent escalations, a full quarter of rental income from two Ohio dispensaries acquired in 2025, including rent from funded tenant allowances at these properties, and rental income from the acquisition of a Kentucky dispensary property on August 4, 2026.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company generated total revenue of approximately $36.4 million, representing a 6.0% decrease compared to $38.7 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease was driven by the loss of rental income from three properties that became available for lease during 2025. The decrease was partially offset by annual contractual rent escalations, a full nine months of rental income from two Ohio dispensaries acquired in 2025, including rent from funded tenant allowances at these properties, and rental income from the acquisition of a dispensary property in Kentucky on August 4, 2026.

The decline in AFFO for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily driven by the loss of rental income and additional property carrying costs associated with properties available for lease, resulting in decreases of approximately 6.3% and 7.6%, respectively, compared to the corresponding periods in 2025.



Investment Activity
Portfolio and Tenant Updates
The Cannabist Company
The Company continues to monitor developments related to Cannabist and its restructuring proceedings. Below is a summary of the developments during the third quarter of 2026.
The Cannabist Company

In September 2026, the Company entered into agreements providing for the termination of Cannabist's lease at the Company's Aurora, Illinois cultivation facility and the commencement of a new lease with the acquirer of Cannabist's cultivation operations at this facility, each effective upon closing of the related transaction, which remained pending as of September 30, 2026. In connection with these agreements, Cannabist forfeited its security deposit of approximately $100.0 thousand, which the Company recognized as rental income during the three months ended September 30, 2026.

At the Company's other three properties leased to Cannabist, the Company collected all contractual rent due through September 30, 2026. The Company holds aggregate security deposits of approximately $381.5 thousand related to these properties.
PharmaCann

In August 2026, the Company consented to a change in control of PharmaCann Penn LLC, the tenant at the Company's Shamokin, Pennsylvania dispensary property, whereby an affiliate of Cresco Labs Inc. acquired the tenant. In connection with the transaction, Cresco Labs Inc. became a guarantor under the existing lease. The terms of the lease otherwise remained unchanged.

Financing Activity

Revolving Credit Facility

In August 2026, NLCP Operating Partnership, L.P. (the "Operating Partnership") entered into an amendment to its Revolving Credit Facility, amending the Loan and Security Agreement. The amendment (i) maintained aggregate revolving commitment of $90.0 million, with one existing regional bank providing the full commitment under the facility, subject to lender approval for borrowings in excess of $30.0 million in aggregate principal amount; (ii) extended the maturity date by two years from May 6, 2027 to May 6, 2029; (iii) modified the interest rate such that borrowings bear interest at a variable rate equal to the greater of (a) the Prime Rate quoted in The Wall Street Journal (Western Edition) and (b) 6.25%; and (iv) revised the unused line fee provisions.
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company repaid the $7.6 million outstanding under its revolving credit facility and, as of September 30, 2026, had no outstanding borrowings under the $90.0 million facility.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company was in compliance with the covenants under the revolving credit facility agreement.



Dividend
On September 14, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared a third quarter 2026 cash dividend of $0.43 per share of common stock, equivalent to an annualized dividend of $1.72 per share of common stock. The dividend was paid on October 15, 2026, to stockholders of record at the close of business on September 30, 2026, and represents an AFFO payout ratio of 88%.

About NewLake Capital Partners, Inc.

NewLake Capital Partners, Inc. is an internally-managed real estate investment trust that provides real estate capital to state-licensed cannabis operators through sale-leaseback transactions and third-party purchases and funding for build-to-suit projects. NewLake owns a portfolio of 37 properties, including 15 cultivation facilities and 22 dispensaries, primarily leased to single tenants under triple-net lease agreements. For more information, please visit www.newlake.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Information

Funds From Operations

The Company calculates FFO in accordance with the current National Association of Real Estate Investment Trusts (“NAREIT”) definition. NAREIT currently defines FFO as follows: net income (loss) (computed in accordance with GAAP) excluding depreciation and amortization related to real estate, gains and losses from the sale of certain real estate assets, and impairment write-downs of certain real estate assets and investments in entities when the impairment is directly attributable to decreases in the value of depreciable real estate held by an entity. Other REITs may not define FFO in accordance with the NAREIT definition or may interpret the current NAREIT definition differently and therefore the Company’s computation of FFO may not be comparable to such other REITs.

Adjusted Funds From Operations

The Company calculates AFFO by starting with FFO and adjusting for non-cash and certain non-recurring transactions, including non-cash components of compensation expense and the effect of provisions for credit loss. Other REITs may not define AFFO in the same manner and therefore the Company’s calculation of AFFO may not be comparable to such other REITs. You should not consider FFO and AFFO to be alternatives to net income as a reliable measure of our operating performance; nor should you consider FFO and AFFO to be alternatives to cash flows from operating, investing or financing activities (as defined by GAAP) as measures of liquidity.
