Pioneer Power Announces a 41.7% Year-to-Date Increase in Backlog to $17.9 Million as of September 30, 2026, and Provides a Business Update

 

●Gross Margin Expanded to 23.1%, Compared to 9.3% in Prior Year Quarter
●Operating Loss of $2.8 Million, Inclusive of a $1.4 Million Non-Cash, Non-Recurring Impairment of Software Development Costs, Compared to an Operating Loss of $1.4 Million in Prior Year Quarter
●Non-GAAP Operating Income* of $321,000, Compared to a non-GAAP Operating Loss of $196,000 in Prior Year Quarter
●Backlog of $17.9 Million, Up 41.7% as Compared to $12.6 Million at Year-End 2025
●PRYMUS Quote Pipeline Exceeds $200 Million, with Opportunities Progressing Toward Potential Near-Term Awards
●First PRYMUS System Scheduled for Delivery by End of November, with Second System Scheduled for Delivery by End of December; Timing Reflects Customer Site Readiness
●Reaffirms Expectation for Revenue of Approximately $15 Million in Second Half of 2026, Representing More than 60% Growth Over the First Half of 2026

 

FORT LEE, N.J., Nov. 17, 2026 /BusinessWire/ — Pioneer Power Solutions, Inc. (Nasdaq: PPSI) (“Pioneer” or the “Company”), a leader in the design, manufacture, service and integration of distributed energy resources, power generation equipment and mobile electric vehicle (“EV”) charging solutions, today provided a business update and announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Our PRYMUS on-site power system continues to gain traction as customers look for reliable power solutions that can be deployed in months rather than years,” said Nathan Mazurek, CEO of Pioneer. “Our pipeline of active PRYMUS quotes is now more than $200 million. Since last quarter, several of these opportunities have continued to advance and are approaching potential awards, and we expect the first of these potential awards to come from the military, hospitality and modular data center markets. We are focused on converting this pipeline of opportunities into orders, which we believe represents a significant growth opportunity for Pioneer before the end of 2026.

 

“The first PRYMUS system is scheduled for delivery by the end of November, followed by the second by the end of December. The timing of these deliveries has shifted due to customer site readiness.

 

“Our third quarter results reflect an improvement in operating performance, even as the timing of certain shipments shifted from the third quarter into the fourth quarter. We recorded GAAP operating loss of $2.8 million, however, non-GAAP operating income was $321,000, compared to a non-GAAP operating loss of $196,000 in the prior year quarter. This improvement was driven in part by stronger gross margins and our continued focus on managing costs. Gross margins increased to 23.1%, compared to 9.3% in the prior year quarter, reflecting a meaningful improvement in the profitability of our business.

 

 

 

 

“We believe the broader macro case for distributed power continues to strengthen. The gap between the demand for power and the supply available from the traditional grid keeps widening, and customers across a range of industries are finding it harder to secure the power they need on the timelines they need it. PRYMUS is well positioned to address this demand by providing reliable, scalable power where and when it is needed. With a growing pipeline and customer opportunities continuing to advance, we believe PRYMUS has the potential to become a significant growth driver for Pioneer.”

 

Q3 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Revenue was $5.1 million, as compared to $6.9 million for the same quarter in 2025.
●Gross profit was $1.2 million, or a gross margin of 23.1%, as compared to $640,000, or a gross margin of 9.3%, for the same quarter in 2025.
●Operating loss from continuing operations was $2.8 million, inclusive of a non-recurring, non-cash impairment charge of $1.4 million related to software development costs, as compared to an operating loss from continuing operations of $1.4 million for the same quarter in 2025.
●Non–GAAP operating income* from continuing operations, which excludes corporate overhead expenses, research and development expenses, depreciation and amortization expenses and non-recurring costs and professional fees, was $321,000, an improvement of $517,000, as compared to a non-GAAP operating loss from continuing operations of $196,000 for the same quarter in 2025.
●Net loss was $2.1 million, or $(0.19) per basic and diluted share, inclusive of a gain of $792,000 related to the redemption of the Company’s preferred equity interests in its equity method investment, as compared to a net loss of $2.4 million, or $(0.21) per basic and diluted share, inclusive of a loss from discontinued operations of $580,000, in the year ago quarter.
●Backlog of $17.9 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $12.6 million as of December 31, 2025 and $18.4 million as of June 30, 2026.

 

*A reconciliation between GAAP and non-GAAP measures is provided below. The non-GAAP measures should not be considered an alternative to GAAP measures as an indicator of the Company’s operating performance.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenue

 

Revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $5.1 million, a decrease of 26.6%, as compared to $6.9 million during the third quarter of last year, primarily due to a decrease in sales and rentals in the Company’s suite of mobile EV charging solutions, e-Boost.

 

Gross Profit/Margin

 

Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.2 million, or a 23.1% gross margin, compared to gross profit of $640,000, or a 9.3% gross margin, for the same period in 2025. The increase in gross margin was primarily attributable to improved operating efficiencies associated with the sale of the Company’s mobile EV charging solutions, e-Boost.

 

 

 

 

Operating Loss from Continuing Operations

 

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, operating loss from continuing operations was $2.8 million, as compared to an operating loss from continuing operations of $1.4 million for the same period in 2025, primarily due to a non-recurring, non-cash impairment charge of $1.4 million recognized during the three months ended September 30, 2026 related to software development costs.

 

Net Loss

 

Net loss was $2.1 million, or $(0.19) per basic and diluted share, as compared to a net loss of $2.4 million, or $(0.21) per basic and diluted share, inclusive of a loss from discontinued operations of $580,000, for the same period last year. Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 included a gain of $792,000 related to the redemption of the Company’s preferred equity interests in its equity method investment.

 

Balance Sheet

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $9.4 million of cash on hand and working capital of $17.0 million, compared to $15.0 million of cash on hand and working capital of $20.7 million as of December 31, 2025. The Company had no bank debt as of September 30, 2026.

 

Second Half 2026 Outlook

 

Management reaffirms its expectation for revenue of approximately $15.0 million for the second half of 2026, representing growth of more than 60% as compared to the first half of 2026.

Non-GAAP Measures

 

In addition to disclosing financial results in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”), this document references certain non-GAAP financial measures. The Company defines non-GAAP operating income (loss) from continuing operations as GAAP operating income (loss) from continuing operations excluding corporate overhead expenses, research and development expenses, depreciation and amortization expenses, and non-recurring costs and professional fees. We believe these non-GAAP financial measures provide investors with useful supplemental information about our operating performance and enable comparison of financial trends and results between periods where certain items may vary, independent of business performance.

 

The Company’s management uses non-GAAP operating income (loss) from continuing operations (a) as a measure of operating performance, (b) for planning and forecasting in future periods, and (c) in communications with the Company’s board of directors concerning the Company’s financial performance. The Company’s presentation of this non-GAAP measure is not necessarily comparable to other similarly titled captions of other companies due to different methods of calculation and should not be used by investors as a substitute for or alternative to any measure of financial performance calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP. Instead, management believes this non-GAAP measure should be used to supplement the Company’s financial measures derived in accordance with U.S. GAAP in order to provide a more complete understanding of the trends affecting the business.

About Pioneer Power Solutions, Inc.

 

Pioneer Power Solutions, Inc. is a leader in the design, manufacture, integration, service of distributed energy resources, power generation equipment and mobile electric charging solutions for applications in the utility, industrial and commercial markets. To learn more about Pioneer, please visit its website at www.pioneerpowersolutions.com.

 

e-Boost is Pioneer’s portfolio of smart, mobile EV charging solutions designed for speed, flexibility, and sustainability. Since its launch in November 2021, e-Boost has established itself as the market leader, delivering mobile, off-grid charging solutions with an extensive range of platforms. Utilized by electric bus and truck manufacturers, fleet management companies, municipalities, and EV infrastructure providers, e-Boost is setting the standard for innovative, all-inclusive EV charging solutions. To learn more about Pioneer’s e-Boost, please visit its website at www.pioneer-emobility.com.

 

PRYMUS is Pioneer’s advanced power systems and controls platform focused on delivering resilient, intelligent, and scalable energy solutions for utility, industrial, and critical infrastructure applications. PRYMUS supports customers through innovative engineering, system integration, and power management technologies designed to improve reliability, operational efficiency, and grid performance.

 

PowerJunction is Pioneer’s distributed energy and infrastructure solutions platform, providing customers with flexible and sustainable power solutions for standby, prime, and mobile power applications. PowerJunction supports a wide range of commercial, industrial, utility, and infrastructure projects through integrated power generation, energy management, and deployment capabilities.
